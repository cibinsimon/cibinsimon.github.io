How I work

  • Start from the business outcome

    Requirements come before diagrams. I spend time with stakeholders until the goal is clear, then design the simplest system that meets it.

  • Design for the second year

    Launch day is the start. I plan for monitoring, maintenance and change so the system is still easy to work on long after go-live.

  • Stay close to the code

    I design and I build. Writing code on the platform keeps my architecture decisions honest and practical for the team.

  • Grow teams with clear ownership

    Teams scale when everyone knows what they own. I hire carefully, set shared standards and give leads room to lead.
